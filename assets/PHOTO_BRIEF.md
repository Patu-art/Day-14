# Photo brief for Strange Brew

This is an independent portfolio demo. All on-site graphics are original illustrations, not photos of the business.

For a client-approved version, request rights-cleared photos of the entrance and patio, the red seating area, the rear live-music space, the bar and the current drinks. Request confirmation of event details and opening hours.

Keep approved photographs inside assets/ and reference relative filenames in the HTML. Do not download venue photographs from Instagram or other websites without permission.
